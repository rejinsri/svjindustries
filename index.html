About Us

Trusted Engineering Partner Since 2010

S.V.J INDUSTRIES was established in 2010 by Mr. G. Sisubalan, a seasoned engineering professional with over 12 years of hands-on expertise in precision manufacturing. What began as a focused machining unit has grown into a trusted precision engineering organisation serving reputed clients across Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.

With a combined industry experience spanning over 16 years, our team is well-trained in studying concept reports, developing engineering drawings, and manufacturing components precisely to customer specifications — delivered within agreed timelines, without compromise.

What We Do

Our Services & Capabilities

Comprehensive precision engineering solutions designed to meet the exacting demands of modern manufacturing — on time, every time.

01

Jigs & Fixtures

Custom-designed jigs and fixtures ensuring dimensional accuracy, repeatability, and productivity in component manufacturing.

02

SPM Machines

Design and fabrication of Special Purpose Machines tailored to specific production requirements, enhancing automation and throughput.

03

Press Tool Manufacturing

High-precision press tools for sheet metal forming, blanking, piercing, and bending — engineered for durability and tight tolerances.

04

Tool Room & Job Work

Comprehensive tool room services including precision machining of components for both tooling and production requirements.

05

Automobile Components

Precision-machined automobile components manufactured to OEM specifications, supporting the automotive supply chain reliably.

06

Gauges

Custom GO/NO-GO, plug, ring, and profile gauges for in-process and final inspection across various industries and tolerances.

07

Fabrication Works

Structural and precision fabrication — welding, cutting, bending, and assembly for industrial and custom applications.
